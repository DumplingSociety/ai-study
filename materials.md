| Months 1–2 | [CS229 Autumn 2018 lectures (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMiGQp3WXShtMGgzqpfVfbU) | Andrew Ng's ML course, 20 lectures |

| Months 1–2 | [CS229 course site](https://cs229.stanford.edu/) | Syllabi with problem sets and solutions |

| Month 5 | [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) | Reference C/C++ inference engine |

| Optional | [Stanford CS224N (NLP)](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/) | Deeper NLP and transformers |
